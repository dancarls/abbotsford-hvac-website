These are the citations that move rankings most in Canada. Do them in order.

- [ ] **Google Business Profile** — https://business.google.com

## Tips that save hours

1. **Use a password manager.** You'll create 40+ accounts. Bitwarden or 1Password.
2. **Use a single dedicated email** for all directory submissions (e.g., `directories@abbotsfordhvac.ca`) so verification emails funnel to one place.
3. **Batch verification emails.** Many directories email you to confirm — batch them for a Sunday hour.
4. **Take screenshots** of each completed listing. Save to a folder — proof + reminder for annual updates.
5. **Add a "Citations" tab to a Google Sheet** tracking: site, URL of your listing, date submitted, date verified, screenshot filename.
6. **Do NOT use citation-building services** that promise "500 citations for $99." They submit to low-quality sites and can trigger Google penalties.

## When to re-check citations

- Every 6 months, run through your list — check that phone number and hours are still correct
- If you change phone number (don't), update every citation the same week
- If you eventually get an address for GBP, go back through and add it to every citation

- **Weeks 1–4:** Citations get indexed. Some show up in Google in 2–3 weeks, others in 8+.
- **Months 2–3:** Cumulative signal starts moving rankings — usually visible in `hvac abbotsford` position (currently 21) creeping toward page 1.
- **Month 4+:** If GBP is live too, map-pack appearances start.

Citations alone won't get you to #1. They're the *foundation* that lets everything else — content, backlinks, GBP — actually work.
