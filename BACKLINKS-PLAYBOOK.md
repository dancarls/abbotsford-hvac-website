# Abbotsford HVAC — Backlink Outreach Playbook

**Goal:** Earn 10–20 real Canadian backlinks in the next 6 months. That's enough to compete with local HVAC sites already ranking page 1.

**Rule #1:** No PBNs, no paid-link networks, no comment spam, no "500 backlinks for $79" services. Google detects these instantly and rankings tank.

**Rule #2:** Every backlink you earn should either (a) come from a real Canadian site with a real audience, or (b) come from a Fraser Valley business/organization where the link makes sense.

---

## The 3 categories that work for local HVAC

### 1. Partnership backlinks (highest ROI, easiest to get)
Businesses that serve the same customers but don't compete:
- Real estate agents (buyers ask about HVAC systems constantly)
- Home inspectors (report includes HVAC recommendations)
- Insurance brokers (home insurance = HVAC risk)
- Roofers, electricians, general contractors
- Property management companies
- Solar installers
- Homebuilders

### 2. Resource-page / directory backlinks (medium ROI)
Local pages listing "Fraser Valley trades" or "Abbotsford home services":
- Chamber member directories (also citations)
- Local blogs with resource lists
- Government/municipal contractor lists
- HomeStars pro profiles with links

### 3. Content-driven backlinks (highest ceiling, slowest)
- Guest posts on Fraser Valley lifestyle blogs
- HARO / Qwoted answers (journalists quote you)
- Original research (your own BC rebate deep-dive — like the one already on your site — attracts links)
- Local news mentions

## HARO / Qwoted quick-start

Every day, journalists post queries needing expert sources. Home & garden, real estate, and personal finance queries often ask HVAC-adjacent questions.

**Sign up:**
- HARO — https://www.helpareporter.com (US-focused, but Canadian outlets ask)
- Qwoted — https://www.qwoted.com (broader)

**Filter for:** Home, real estate, personal finance, energy, sustainability, HVAC, appliances

**Respond within 3 hours** — journalists get 100+ replies, first 10 usually get read.

**Response format:**
- 100–150 words max
- Concrete numbers and examples (this is what wins)
- Include: your name, "Dan at AbbotsfordHVAC.ca", one-line credential ("Fraser Valley HVAC information site with real cost data from local installers"), a link
- Sign off with contact + link

**Example response prompt:** "What's a common mistake homeowners make when replacing their furnace?"

> One of the biggest mistakes I see Fraser Valley homeowners make is sizing a replacement furnace based on the old unit rather than the current home. Homes get renovated — added insulation, new windows, finished basements — and the heat load changes. A furnace sized for a 1985 home layout will short-cycle in that home post-renovation, wearing out 30% faster and heating unevenly. Always insist on a Manual J load calculation (~$150 extra) before signing on a replacement. It's the difference between 15 years of quiet operation and 8 years of expensive callbacks.
>
> — Dan, AbbotsfordHVAC.ca (Fraser Valley HVAC information + installer dispatch), https://www.abbotsfordhvac.ca

## What to send weekly

**Week 1:** Send 5 partnership pitches (Template 1) to real estate agents and home inspectors.
**Week 2:** Send 5 resource-page pitches (Template 2) to Fraser Valley blogs.
**Week 3:** Sign up for HARO/Qwoted; respond to 3 relevant queries.
**Week 4:** Send 2 guest post pitches (Template 3) to Fraser Valley Current or Abbotsford News.

Repeat monthly. 20 pitches/month × 15% response rate × 30% conversion = ~1 link/month. Compounds fast.

## Realistic response rates

- **Cold partnership pitch:** ~10-20% reply, ~5% link
- **Resource-page pitch:** ~15-25% reply, ~10% link (best ROI)
- **Guest post pitch:** ~5-10% reply, ~3% conversion (but 1 link is high-quality)
- **HARO/Qwoted:** ~1 in 20 responses gets used, but usage = real editorial link

Expect: 8–15 real backlinks in 6 months if you send 20 pitches/month consistently.

---

## What NOT to do — the traps

- ❌ **Buying links.** Fiverr, upwork, "SEO packages" — Google spots them, tanks rankings.
- ❌ **Guest-posting on random low-quality sites** just for a link. Google devalues these ~2020+.
- ❌ **Directory spam.** Only submit to citations from the CITATIONS-CHECKLIST.md. Random 50-citation packs = penalty risk.
- ❌ **Comment spam.** Zero SEO value, high risk.
- ❌ **Reciprocal link exchanges at scale** ("link to me, I link to you" across 20 sites). Small-scale, natural reciprocals with actual partners are fine.
- ❌ **Anchor text over-optimization.** If every link says "HVAC Abbotsford" it looks unnatural. Use varied anchors: "AbbotsfordHVAC.ca", "Dan at AbbotsfordHVAC", "this Fraser Valley guide", plain URL.
